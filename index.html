01

EU Tender Intelligence Agent

Turns official TED notices and lots into traceable supplier qualification, change intelligence and grounded answers.

  • Live anonymous TED Search API v3 discovery and structured lot processing
  • Deterministic bid / no-bid checks with evidence-linked decisions
  • 15 recorded real TED notices and 30 curated retrieval scenarios with a notice-grouped holdout
  • Versioned eval gates, adversarial grounding regressions and privacy-safe correlated agent traces

The public app runs live search and deterministic assessment; persistent RAG and agent execution are reproducible locally. The evaluation corpus is source-derived portfolio evidence—not general AI accuracy. Azure infrastructure is prepared, not claimed as deployed.

03

Olist Delivery Delay Predictor

A server-side relative risk scorer developed with point-in-time features and chronological evaluation on Brazilian e-commerce orders.

PR-AUC
6.32%
ROC-AUC
63.44%
Top 10%
107 / 620
  • 96,470 delivered orders with a held-out 14,471-order final benchmark
  • Leakage-safe historical features and sequential model selection
  • Logistic regression retained after comparison with XGBoost, CatBoost and blends
  • Portable TypeScript inference checked against the Python reference

Predictive quality is modest: the highest-risk 10% found 107 of 620 late orders at 7.4% precision. The interface presents the score as relative risk, not a calibrated probability.

04

Finland Geospatial AI

0.5 m segmentation of official NLS orthophotos with Topographic Database labels, disjoint map-sheet splits and GeoTIFF inference.

mIoU
0.6652
Macro Dice
0.7742
Test patches
96
  • 576 256 px patches from five NLS map sheets at native 0.5 m RGB resolution
  • Exact EPSG:3067 raster/vector alignment, 512 m split buffers and automated leakage checks
  • Two U-Nets and pretrained SegFormer-B0 tracked in MLflow; selection used validation only
  • Single hash-locked test, boundary/error visuals, ECE / risk coverage and CPU GeoTIFF inference

Water reached 0.9636 IoU, while open-natural land reached 0.3300. The older Sentinel-2 v1 is unchanged at 0.3982 mIoU / 0.4743 macro Dice. Its sensor, labels, classes and regions differ, so the scores cannot be compared directly.

Decision science · Water

05

Helsinki Water — Forecasting + Optimization

A reproducible monthly forecasting and inspection-triage study built from real City of Helsinki public-property water meters.

ETS MASE
0.793
Coverage (90% target)
97.92%
Signals
3
  • 864 official Nuuka observations: eight properties × 108 complete months
  • Seven-origin expanding backtests, sealed 2018 and four forecasting approaches
  • Conformal intervals, honest statistical anomaly semantics and failure analysis
  • OR-Tools CP-SAT inspection allocation with cost, budget and uncertainty sensitivity

The 90% intervals were over-conservative, SARIMA beat the development-selected ETS ex post on property MASE, and base optimization gain was 0%; a binding eight-hour budget produced 14.59%. Signals are not labelled as confirmed leaks.
